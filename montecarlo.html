Simulation Parameters
Each slider controls a key input variable fed into 100,000 Monte Carlo iterations. War Duration uses a log-normal distribution calibrated to Trump/WH estimates. Hormuz Min/Max sets the uniform closure window based on IRGC operational data. Alliance Fragmentation and Regime Collapse are Bernoulli probabilities. Nuclear Escalation is calibrated to the NPEC wargame 8% baseline. Proxy Fronts and Russia Escalation use Poisson and triangular distributions respectively.
